    Why it fell

    There are about 2.2 billion possible passwords of 6 lowercase letters and digits. At 10 billion guesses a second against a fast, unsalted hash, trying them all takes under a second. A random five-word passphrase would take about 90 years at the same speed.

    Better habit: let a password manager create a long, unique password for each account, and turn on multi-factor authentication.
